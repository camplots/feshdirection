3. OUR SERVICE

(a) Assessment and report. Generated from the information you provide. It is indicative only — it is not a quote, not a system design, and not a site survey.

(b) Consultation and photo capture. A short consultation and a guided photo capture. These provide clarity; they are not an assessment of your property by a qualified installer.

(c) Introduction. If you request it, we may make one introduction to an installer.

(d) Independent inspection. Where you proceed with an installer introduced through us, we arrange an independent inspection after installation.

We do not warrant that an inspection will identify all defects or non-compliances.
