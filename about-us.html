WHO YOU'RE TALKING TO

SEVEN YEARS.
THOUSANDS OF APPOINTMENTS.

I've sat across the table from enough homeowners to know the pattern. A slick proposal, an average payback figure, and no way to tell whether any of it is real. Then the panels go on the roof and the homeowner is left working out on their own whether the job was done properly.

Most of the time, it wasn't.

That's why Watts Better exists. Not to sell you solar — to make sure you understand what you're buying before you buy it, and to make sure someone independent checks it afterwards.

WHAT I DO

I'm a solar consultant, not an installer. I don't sell hardware and I don't work for a manufacturer.

For over seven years I've advised on rooftop solar and home batteries, and sat thousands of appointments doing it. I've seen what separates a system that performs for twenty-five years from one that quietly underdelivers from year two — and it's almost never the brand on the panel.

WHAT I FOCUS ON

Solar and home batteries. That's the whole scope.

No hot water, no EV chargers, no heat pumps, no upsells into things you didn't ask about. Depth over breadth — because batteries are where the money, the compliance risk and the complexity now sit, and that's where the expertise needs to be.

HOW I WORK

  • One introduction, if you request it. I don't sell your details to a network of installers.
  • Clarity first. A short chat and a photo capture before any introduction, so we're working from facts.
  • Independent verification. An independent inspector checks the finished install, and you get the report.
  • A disclosed fee. If you proceed with an installer I introduce, they pay me an introducer fee — part of which funds your inspection. HOW WE'RE PAID →

WHAT I WON'T DO

  • Tell you a battery is right for your home when it isn't.
