HOW WE ARE PAID

WHO PAYS,
AND WHY.

THE SOLAR INDUSTRY GENERALLY OPERATES THROUGH PAID LEAD ACQUISITION.

Most people exploring solar and batteries go through one of the major quote-comparison services. Their enquiry is then allocated to several businesses, each of which pays for the opportunity.

The sales consultants from those businesses then attempt to contact the homeowner, discuss their situation, and secure the installation.

The homeowner may receive several quotes for different systems, different equipment, and different prices. It can become difficult to work out whether the recommendations are genuinely different because of the household, or simply reflect the preferences and sales approach of each business.

SolarQuotes was sold to Origin Energy, and Solar Choice was acquired by Flow Power. Both remain significant ways for solar businesses to obtain leads, but the underlying process is still based on selling customer enquiries to businesses.

WHAT A LEAD COSTS AN INSTALLER

If an installer is buying leads at around $80 each and converts roughly one in seven into an installation, the cost of the leads alone is around $560 for one completed installation.

That does not include the consultant’s time, travel, consultations, follow-up, or the broader costs of running the business.

A conservative estimate is:

Around $560 for the leads
Around $700 for the consultant’s time
Around $250 for other business costs
$1,500THAT PUTS THE COST OF SECURING ONE INSTALLATION AT APPROXIMATELY $1,500

The installer is already carrying this cost when acquiring customers through the usual lead process.

HOW WATTS BETTER WORKS

Watts Better does not sell a customer’s details to several installers.

The customer first uses the analysis and is required to complete a 15-minute call with us before any introduction is made.

That gives us the opportunity to discuss the customer’s circumstances and provide extra guidance before they decide whether they would like an installer introduction.

$1,500COST OF SECURING ONE INSTALLATION
$750INTRODUCER FEE

If the customer asks us to make an introduction and then proceeds with that installer, the installer will pay Watts Better an introducer fee of $750.

That is approximately half of the conservative cost an installer may otherwise incur acquiring and converting the same installation through the normal lead process.

WHY THERE IS NO ADDITIONAL CHARGE TO CLIENTS

The introducer fee is paid from the installer’s existing cost of acquiring work.

NO ADDITIONAL CHARGE

It is not an additional charge to clients.

The client does not pay Watts Better for the analysis, the 15-minute call, or the installer introduction.

If the client proceeds with an installer introduced through Watts Better, there is also no additional charge to the client for the independent inspection.

The client pays the installer for the system the installer designs, quotes, and installs.

WHERE THE FEE GOES

The independent inspection costs approximately $300.

That amount comes from the introducer fee and funds the inspection and report provided to the client.

The remaining amount allows Watts Better to provide the analysis, advice, and coordination involved in the service.
