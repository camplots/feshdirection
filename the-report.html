A CLEARER STARTING POINT

Most solar conversations begin with a system size.

  • How many panels?
  • What size battery?
  • What will it cost?

Those questions cannot be answered properly without first understanding how your home uses electricity.

Your report starts with your energy information and explains what it may mean for solar PV and home battery options.

It does not assume that:

  • The largest system is the best system
  • Every home needs a battery
  • A generic payback figure applies to your household
  • An installer's first recommendation is automatically the right one

It gives you a clearer starting point.

WHAT YOUR REPORT EXPLAINS

YOUR ENERGY PROFILE

Your report explains how your home uses electricity and when that usage occurs.

It considers the relationship between:

  • Daily energy use
  • Daytime consumption
  • Evening consumption
  • Peak-period usage
  • Grid imports
  • Solar generation, where information is available
  • Energy exported to the grid

The important question is not only how much electricity your home uses.

It is when your home needs it.

YOUR TARIFF

The cost of electricity depends on when it is used.

Your report explains the tariff information available from your bill and how it affects the value of solar and battery storage.

It may identify:

  • Higher-cost usage periods
  • Lower-cost usage periods
  • Supply charges
  • Feed-in credits
  • The value of using solar directly
  • The potential value of storing solar for later use

A battery may be more useful in one household than another, even when both homes use a similar amount of electricity.

The tariff and usage pattern matter.

YOUR SOLAR OPTIONS

The report considers solar PV pathways based on your available energy information.

It may compare different system sizes and explain the potential effect on:

  • Solar generation
  • Grid imports
  • Solar self-consumption
  • Exported energy
  • Estimated savings
  • Indicative payback
  • Longer-term value

The final system design still needs to be confirmed by an installer after assessing your property.

YOUR BATTERY OPTIONS

A battery is not automatically the right choice.

The report considers whether your usage pattern creates a reasonable opportunity to store solar during the day and use it later when grid electricity is more expensive.

Where a battery appears suitable, the report explains why.

Where solar-only or battery-ready solar appears stronger, it says so.

A battery should earn its place in the recommendation.

YOUR OPTIONS COMPARED

Your report compares different solar PV and home battery pathways and identifies the options that appear most relevant to your circumstances.

These may include:

  • Solar PV only
  • Solar PV with a home battery
  • A smaller solar system
  • A larger solar system
  • Battery-ready solar
  • Adding a battery later
  • No immediate equipment change

The options are considered against factors such as:

  • Upfront cost
  • Estimated savings
  • Self-consumption
  • Indicative payback
  • Battery utilisation
  • Exported energy
  • Longer-term value

The report is not designed to make every household buy more equipment.

It is designed to show which options deserve further consideration.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Your report gives you questions to take into an installer conversation, including:

  • What system size is actually justified by my usage?
  • Is the battery sized to my evening demand?
  • What tariff has been used in the modelling?
  • What happens if my feed-in tariff changes?
  • Is the battery on the approved product list?
  • Does the installer hold the relevant battery accreditation?
  • Is a switchboard upgrade included?
  • What assumptions support the claimed payback?
  • What is included in the installation price?
  • What happens if the final design differs from the proposal?

YOUR ASSUMPTIONS

Every analysis has limits.

Your report identifies the assumptions used, including:

  • The bill period analysed
  • The tariff information available
  • Solar generation assumptions
  • Battery assumptions
  • Estimated system performance
  • Information that still needs to be confirmed
  • Matters requiring an installer site assessment

You should be able to see not only the result, but also what sits behind it.

THE CHAT CLARIFIES THE HOME.

Your bill can tell us a great deal.

It can show when your home uses electricity, what you pay for it and whether solar or battery storage may improve the position.

But it cannot confirm everything about your property.

It cannot reliably confirm:

  • Available roof space
  • Roof condition
  • Detailed orientation and shading
  • Meter box condition
  • Switchboard configuration
  • Existing inverter condition
  • Existing battery brand or model
  • Battery location
  • Cable routes
  • Structural requirements
  • Whether the proposed system can be safely installed

That is why the next step is a 15-minute chat with me.

During the chat, we can:

  • Walk through your report
  • Explain the main findings
  • Discuss whether solar, a battery or both appear worth exploring
  • Talk through your existing system, if you have one
  • Identify anything that needs clarification
  • Explain what the bill cannot show
  • Guide you through the Photo Capture
  • Answer your questions before you decide what happens next

This is not an installer sales appointment.

It is not a commitment to buy anything.

The report explains the numbers. The chat clarifies the home. The introduction is your choice.

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER INSTALLATION

If you proceed with an installer introduced through Watts Better, we arrange an independent post-installation inspection.

The inspection provides a separate assessment of the completed installation and gives you a formal report.

The stages are different:

  • The Report explains your energy numbers.
  • The 15-minute chat clarifies the questions those numbers cannot answer.
  • The Photo Capture records useful information about the physical starting point.
  • The Installer Introduction is made only if you request it.
  • The Independent Inspection checks the completed installation afterwards.

WHAT THE REPORT IS — AND IS NOT

THE REPORT IS

  • An independent starting point
  • A bill-based analysis
  • A comparison of solar PV and home battery pathways
  • A record of the assumptions used
  • A preparation tool for an installer conversation
  • A way to identify the questions that still need answering

THE REPORT IS NOT

  • A final system design
  • A site assessment
  • An electrical inspection
  • A structural assessment
  • A formal quote
  • Financial advice
  • A guarantee of savings or payback
  • A replacement for installer certification

The installer remains responsible for the final site assessment, system design, equipment selection, installation, certification and network requirements.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Q

Do I need to speak with an installer to receive the report?

No. You can review your report and book a chat without requesting an installer introduction.

Q

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The report and chat are intended to help you make a more informed decision. You can continue researching, use your own installer or decide not to proceed.

Q

Does the report always recommend a battery?

No. A battery may be suitable for some households and less suitable for others. If the analysis suggests solar-only or battery-ready solar is the stronger option, the report will explain that.

Q

Can the report confirm whether my roof is suitable?

No. The report helps identify the energy pathway worth exploring. An installer must still confirm roof condition, available space, shading, structural requirements and installation feasibility.

Q

Why book a 15-minute chat?

Because a bill explains your energy pattern, but it cannot show the condition or configuration of your home. The chat gives you an opportunity to understand the report, ask questions and prepare the Photo Capture before deciding whether you want an installer introduction.

Q

Will my information be shared with an installer?

Not unless you expressly request an introduction. You can receive your report, speak with me and complete the Photo Capture without your information being shared with an installer.

Q

Does Watts Better offer other products?

No. Watts Better focuses on solar PV and home batteries.
